          Lab Mode

          Off (default): every table is used together regardless of shape — a mix of 4-seat and 2-seat tables all get filled as one room. On: pair desks and group tables are kept as two separate layouts, and the Group Tables / Pair Up toggle switches which set is in use (for a room built with two distinct physical arrangements, like a lab with benches and desks).

          Potential Pairings

          Pick a student and tick who they'd be a good match with — ticks work for already-seated students too, so updates here take effect on the next 🎲 Generate rather than the current seating.

          Friend Groups

          For a bigger friendship group — sitting next to one of them is still totally fine, this just stops the whole group clumping into one corner of the room. Different from Proximity Rules (which govern who sits directly next to whom).
